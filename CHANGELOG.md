# Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first. Your installed version is on the Universal Offer Hub card in `chrome://extensions`. To update, see [Install](./README.md#install). From 3.4.0 on, every update here gets a new version number.

## 3.4.1 (22 Nov 2026)

- Capital One Shopping: Home Page Activation Bonus offers ("Get $100 back when you spend $330") are now saved, next to the store's other rates. Before, only the store's regular rate was saved, and a store with only a bonus offer counted as couldn't read.

## 3.4.0 (22 Nov 2026)

- New opt-in toggle, **Auto-load Capital One Shopping offers when I open it**: opening capitaloneshopping.com reads its offers in a background tab, and your own tab is left alone.
- Amex and Capital One cards that end in the same digits (yours, or another person's login) now keep their own offers, nickname and order.
- The dashboard's card filter groups cards by owner: issuer, then owner, then card.
- Amex auto-add opens your Profile page first, in its own tab, to see whose cards they are.
- The toolbar badge shows 999+ past 999 offers.
- A run's summary names cards it skipped because Add is off in Cards.

## 3.3.0 (21 Nov 2026)

- New source: Capital One Shopping. Its autopilot saves every store's reward, reading for the minutes you choose beside Run.
- Its progress shows in Chrome's side panel, with Stop and save.
- Each of its offers has an Open link that activates the reward the way the site does.
- Update later that day: a Source filter in the dashboard, column filters that stay open while you pick, and columns that keep their width.

## 3.2.0 (2 Nov 2026)

- New opt-in toggle, **Auto-load Capital One offers when I sign in**: every offer on each card is loaded and saved, none activated.
- Update on 17 Nov: Capital One auto-load opens your offers through View all offers on your home page.
- Update on 17 Nov: Amex auto-add starts from Overview, and reads added offers again after Amex renamed its page styles.
- Update on 17 Nov: Chase carousel offers get added too.

## 3.1.0 (31 Oct 2026)

- Chase auto-add on sign-in (opt-in), with its own toggle and Cards link.
- Auto-add runs each bank's own Run Autopilot, in a background tab.
- Inside the 2-hour wait, the bank's page says when the next run can start.

## 3.0.0 (29 Oct 2026)

- Full-page dashboard.
- Ask AI with your own ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini key.
- Amex auto-add on sign-in (opt-in), card by card, top card first.
- Your offers in AI chats, and a one-click Claude Desktop plugin.
